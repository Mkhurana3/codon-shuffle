`amino_acids.csv`: three values for each amino acid, typed in from the papers.

- polar_requirement: Woese et al. 1966 (low = oily, high = likes water)
- hydropathy: Kyte & Doolittle 1982 (high = oily)
- molecular_weight: g/mol

`alphafold_plddt.csv`: AlphaFold's confidence score (pLDDT) for every amino acid of the 22 proteins in notebook 03, downloaded from the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (alphafold.ebi.ac.uk) on 10 Nov 2026. Delete it to download fresh data.
